How It Works

Pipeline
1

Camera Captures Video

Wide-angle lens continuously streams 640x480 frames at zero-latency buffer bypass.

2

Ultrasonic Radar Measures Distance

HC-SR04 sonar pulses every 50ms to detect physical barriers up to 3.0 meters away.

3

Raspberry Pi 5 AI Inference

YOLOv8 detects 92 custom obstacles and partitions field of view into Left, Center, and Right.

4

Actionable Audio Guidance

Non-blocking spatial voice commands: "Obstacle on left, go right" or "Path clear ahead".
